Run the LINT operation on the Obsidian vault at ~/Documents/Day to day/. Use the Obsidian CLI:

1. obsidian orphans vault="Day to day" total
2. obsidian unresolved vault="Day to day" total
3. obsidian deadends vault="Day to day" total
4. obsidian tasks vault="Day to day" todo total
5. Count root-level files missing type frontmatter
6. Compare with previous health report at ~/Documents/Day to day/_llm/status/health.md to note improvements or regressions
7. Write updated report to ~/Documents/Day to day/_llm/status/health.md
8. Append to ~/Documents/Day to day/_llm/log.md
9. Open in Obsidian: obsidian open vault="Day to day" path="_llm/status/health.md"
